About us

A trusted manufacturer built on 25+ years of dependable delivery.

Founded in Pondicherry in 1998, KLAS manufactures plastic components for the automotive industry, with parts going into vehicles from leading brands including Hyundai and Kia.

Every part is moulded and checked on our own floor, by operators who run the machines and inspect quality at each stage. That hands-on discipline is why our customers have stayed with us for decades.
